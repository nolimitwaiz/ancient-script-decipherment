\section{TLA pseudo-documents (the honest caveat)}
The official TLA exports carry no text IDs and are not text-ordered
(contiguous same-date runs $\approx$ 1 sentence). \texttt{doc\_id} is
therefore the \textbf{dating cohort}: all sentences sharing the exact
(dateNotBefore, dateNotAfter, authors) triple. A real text's sentences always
share these values, so no text can straddle partitions; cohorts over-merge
(95/34/130 groups), which errs strict, never leaky. Obtaining real text IDs
from the TLA maintainers is an open gap that would upgrade cohort-held-out to
true document-held-out.

\section{Splits and freezing}
Seed 1234, ratios 80/10/10 by record weight over whole groups; every split
ships \texttt{split\_info.json}; all 19 test partitions are SHA-256-frozen in
the committed manifest. The guard now \emph{enforces} freezing: reading a
frozen test file re-hashes it (tamper $\Rightarrow$ hard error) and write-mode
opens of frozen files are refused --- tested in both directions.

Headline scheme = \texttt{dedup}: cohort/document-held-out plus cross-split
near-duplicate removal (normalized edit distance $\le 0.1$; exact-hash plus
banded Levenshtein behind a bag-of-characters lower bound).

Also materialized: \texttt{sign\_heldout} (25 mid-frequency sign types held
out entirely; 197/335 test sentences for Earlier/Late), and
\texttt{period\_heldout} (century buckets; Earlier + Demotic --- Late Egyptian
has only 8 buckets and the scheme is dropped there with a note).
\texttt{site\_heldout}: no Phase 1 corpus carries site metadata.
\texttt{random} exists as a labeled diagnostic only.

\section{Environment note}
The Desktop/iCloud problem from Phase 0 \emph{recurred}: overnight, 6{,}760
venv files were re-flagged macOS-hidden, which makes Python $\ge$3.12 skip
\texttt{.pth} files and silently breaks imports. Durable fix: \texttt{.venv}
is now a symlink to \texttt{\textasciitilde/.venvs/glyphos} (outside iCloud),
matching \texttt{data $\to$ \textasciitilde/dev/glyphos-data}. No mutable tool
state remains inside iCloud.

\section{Next: Phase 2 --- rendering engine}

\section*{Addendum (second pass, 2026-08-02): frontier downloads}
Every remaining openly-obtainable corpus was downloaded and integrated ---
the census now counts \textbf{11 ingested corpora and 26 frozen test
partitions}: First1KGreek (392{,}478 passages, 28.8M tokens; the cluster
deferral was reversed --- parsing cost 15\,s), CDLI cuneiform (132{,}210
tablets with catalogue language/period; categorical period-held-out), and
the first machine-readable \textbf{Meroitic} corpus (Otten \&
Anastasopoulos 2025; 18{,}103 lines; its bundled pretrained embeddings are
quarantined under the sealed constraint). Mayan and Libyco-Berber have no
open bulk source (MHD application-gated; the LBI database is defunct and
its domain squatted --- recoverable from pre-2020 Wayback captures);
concrete unblock paths are filed in \texttt{docs/data\_gaps.md}. Dedup for
the two large monolingual LM corpora is exact-duplicate-only (Greek $-$617,
CDLI $-$1{,}891); TLA headline splits keep the full near-duplicate pass.
